PROGRAM 01

Kids Program

The Kīpapa Kids Program uses Jiu Jitsu to help children grow in confidence, discipline, respect, and resilience. In our small classes, every child receives personal attention and encouragement while learning the value of hard work, humility, and perseverance. We believe the lessons learned on the mats carry into the home, the classroom, and everyday life, helping shape strong kids with strong character.

PROGRAM 02

Adults Program

The Kīpapa Adults Program is designed for students of all experience levels. Through personalized coaching and a structured curriculum, students build a strong foundation in core Jiu-Jitsu positions, techniques, and concepts before progressing into live training and sparring. Our small class sizes create a welcoming environment where every student can grow in skill, confidence, and problem-solving on and off the mats.

PROGRAM 03

Nogi Program

The Kīpapa Nogi Program is designed for students of all experience levels who want to develop practical, fast-paced Jiu Jitsu without the traditional gi. The curriculum emphasizes modern Nogi techniques, positions, and strategies while building strong fundamentals through personalized coaching and live training. Our small class sizes create an environment where every student can grow in skill, confidence, and performance on the mats.
